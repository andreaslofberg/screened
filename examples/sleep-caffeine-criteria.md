# Screening criteria (title/abstract stage)

Review working title: Afternoon caffeine and subsequent sleep in humans (SYNTHETIC DEMO)

Question (PICO):
- Population: humans (any age), not animals
- Intervention / exposure: caffeine, coffee, espresso, tea, or caffeine gum
- Comparator: placebo, decaf, no caffeine, or any
- Outcomes: sleep (latency, duration, quality, PSG, actigraphy, sleep diary)
- Study designs: primary empirical studies with results (trials, observational)

## Inclusion

- Human participants
- Caffeine or caffeinated beverage/gum as an exposure
- At least one sleep outcome (latency, duration, efficiency, quality, PSG, actigraphy, diary)

## Exclusion

- Animal or in vitro only
- No sleep outcome
- Protocols, planned studies, or methods papers without results
- Narrative or systematic reviews (no primary data)

## Title/abstract conventions

- include = retrieve full text, not a final include
- If abstract is missing and title is not a clear mismatch → maybe
- Do not exclude on sample size, quality, or paywall unless listed above
