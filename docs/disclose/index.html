Checklist excerpt (5 lines)

From Art. 50(1) + 50(5):

  1. Does our system interact directly with people?
  2. Do we tell them it’s an AI system (unless truly obvious)?
  3. Is that notice clear and distinguishable?
  4. Is it available at the latest at first interaction?
  5. Does the notice meet accessibility requirements?
